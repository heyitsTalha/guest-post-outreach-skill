# Daily report format

Write headings and prose in `report_language`. Keep emails and answers in the language they will be sent in.

```markdown
# Guest Post Outreach — <Weekday>, <D Month YYYY>
Found <N> new sites today, <N> pitch drafts ready.

## Today's sites
| # | Site | Write-for-us link | AS | Contact | Subject | Draft |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | example.com | https://example.com/write-for-us | 32 | editor@example.com | Guest post idea for … | <draft link> |

## Emails (review, then send yourself)
**<Site>** — To: <email> — Subject: <subject>
> full email body

## Quora / Reddit answers (post yourself)
**Quora** — <question link>
> full answer

## Skipped sites
- domain.com — reason
(or "None")

## Issues
- anything that failed (Gmail, Semrush, tracker write), or "None"

Tracker: <tracker link>
```

## Hinglish example headings

`Aaj <N> guest post sites dhoondi, <N> pitch drafts tayyar.` · `Aaj ki guest post sites` · `Mails (review karke Send karein)` · `Quora / Reddit answers (khud post karein)` · `Skip ki gayi sites` (empty: `Koi nahi`)
